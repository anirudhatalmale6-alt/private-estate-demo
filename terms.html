Terms of Service

These terms govern the use of this website and the Private Client Portal operated by Holland & Gray (“we”, “us”). By using the site or the portal you accept these terms. Membership services are governed additionally by the written agreement issued to each member.

Engagement & membership

Membership is offered following a private consultation and a review of the property, and is confirmed only by a signed agreement. Nothing on this website constitutes an offer of service, a quotation, or a guarantee of availability. We reserve the right to decline an enquiry without providing reasons.

  • Membership applies to the residence or residences named in the agreement.
  • Levels of service, response times and inclusions are as set out in that agreement.
  • Either party may end the arrangement in accordance with the notice period stated in the agreement.

Service requests & quotes

Requests raised through the portal are acknowledged during business hours unless your membership provides for after-hours attendance. Where work requires expenditure beyond the agreed maintenance scope, a quotation is issued to the portal for your approval. No chargeable work proceeds without an approval recorded against that quotation, except where urgent action is required to prevent injury, loss or further damage.

Contractors

Trades and contractors engaged on your behalf are independently licensed and insured. We select, instruct and supervise them, and we review their work. Where a contractor is engaged directly by you, our responsibility is limited to coordination only.

Fees & payment

Membership fees, project fees and disbursements are set out in your agreement and invoiced on the cycle stated there. Prices are not published on this website. Fees are quoted in Australian dollars and are inclusive or exclusive of GST as stated on each invoice.

Liability

Nothing in these terms excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law. Subject to that, our liability arising from the services is limited, at our election, to re-supplying the services or paying the cost of having them re-supplied. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss.

Privacy Policy

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. Discretion is central to our service, and we treat client information accordingly.

What we collect

  • Contact details provided through a consultation enquiry or by a member.
  • Property information: addresses, access arrangements, assets, warranties and service history.
  • Portal content: service requests, messages, uploaded files and photographs, and approvals.
  • Technical information such as log data required to keep the portal secure.

How we use it

Solely to provide and administer the services: arranging attendance, instructing contractors, maintaining records, issuing quotations and invoices, and communicating with you. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use client details for marketing lists.

Information is disclosed to a contractor only to the extent required to complete a specific task — typically the property address, access instruction and scope of works. Contractors engaged by us are bound by confidentiality obligations.

Storage & security

Data is held on servers located in Australia, encrypted in transit and at rest. Portal access is restricted to the account holder and authorised persons nominated in writing. Records are retained for the period required for legal, insurance and warranty purposes, and are then destroyed or de-identified.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask that it be corrected, or raise a complaint about how it has been handled. Requests may be made through the portal or to your estate manager, and are responded to within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may refer the matter to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Cookies

This website uses only the cookies necessary to operate the portal session. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are set.
