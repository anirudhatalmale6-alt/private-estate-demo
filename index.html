Who we serve

We look after homes for people whose time is spent elsewhere.

Principals, professionals and family offices in Melbourne's established suburbs — Toorak, Brighton, Kew, Armadale and the Peninsula — who would rather approve a decision than chase one.

Holland & Gray sits between the residence and everyone who works on it. We hold the keys, the schedule, the records and the standard. You receive one report, one contact, and one invoice.

Scope of care

Everything a residence quietly requires, held in one place.

01

Property Oversight

Scheduled inspections, condition reporting and a standing record of every asset, warranty and service date.

02

Preventative Maintenance

Planned programmes for roofing, drainage, climate systems, pools, gardens and finishes — before they announce themselves.

03

Trades & Contractor Management

Vetted, insured and supervised. Quotes obtained, compared and presented to you with a recommendation.

04

Absentee & Seasonal Care

Homes held to standard while you are away: security checks, deliveries, climate, gardens and arrival preparation.

05

Household Staffing Support

Coordination of housekeepers, gardeners and drivers, with rosters, standards and discreet performance review.

06

Projects & Renovations

Oversight of works from brief to handover, protecting the residence, the timeline and the finish.
