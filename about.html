About us

A small firm, deliberately.

Holland & Gray was founded on a simple observation: the people best placed to look after a fine home are rarely the people best placed to manage it. We do the managing.

Our position

We are not a maintenance company. We are the party a homeowner appoints to hold the standard — then instruct, supervise and answer for everyone who works on the residence.

That distinction matters. A trade is engaged for a task and leaves. An estate manager carries the history of the house: what was replaced and when, which warranty still applies, which contractor did good work in the west wing three winters ago, and what will need attention before the next one.

We take on a limited number of residences each year so that this knowledge stays real rather than filed. Every member has a named contact who has walked their property personally.
