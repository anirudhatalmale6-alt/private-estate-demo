Services & Membership

A standing arrangement, rather than a series of jobs.

Members appoint us once. From that point the residence is inspected, maintained, recorded and reported on to an agreed standard — and every trade who enters it answers to us.

Scope in detail

What a membership covers

01

Inspection & Reporting

A methodical walk of the property against a schedule built for that residence — roof, drainage, envelope, services, grounds and interiors — recorded with photographs and issued to the portal.

02

Preventative Maintenance

Gutters, climate systems, hot water, pools and spas, irrigation, timber and stone finishes, pest and moisture control — all cycled on a calendar rather than attended to in an emergency.

03

Trades & Contractors

Sourcing, credential and insurance checks, attendance on site, quality review and sign-off. Quotes are obtained, compared and presented with our recommendation for your approval.

04

Absentee & Seasonal Care

Security and condition checks while you are away, mail and deliveries, climate and garden management, and the residence prepared for your return.

05

Household Support

Coordination of housekeepers, gardeners, pool technicians and drivers — rosters, standards, and quiet performance review, so the household runs without your involvement.

06

Projects & Renovations

Representation of your interest through works of any scale: scope, tender, programme, site conduct, protection of the residence, defect review and handover.

07

Records & Documents

Warranties, manuals, certificates, insurance schedules, service history and contractor details held in one place and accessible to you at any time.
